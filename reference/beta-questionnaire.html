This questionnaire exists for one reason: to make Being HAware better. Not better in the eyes of the person who wrote it — better in the experience of the person doing it. That person is you. Your honest answers, however uncomfortable, are more valuable than any amount of encouragement.

There are no wrong answers. If something confused you, that is the programme's fault, not yours. If something didn't work for your body, that is important information. If you gave up — tell us when, and why. All of it counts.

Please answer every section you have experience of. Skip any question that genuinely does not apply. Take as long as you need.
